# Paystack escalation kit

## Step 0 — First, rule out the silent killers (5 min, do before emailing)

Log into dashboard.paystack.com and verify ALL of these:

- [ ] **Settings → Compliance**: every section shows a **green checkmark**.
      One incomplete section = automatic indefinite wait.
- [ ] **Check your email spam/promotions folder NOW.** Paystack's reviewers
      often email ONE clarifying question and silently hold the application
      until answered. This is the #1 cause of "lengthy" approvals.
- [ ] **Names match everywhere**: account name = ID name = payout
      bank/M-Pesa name. Even small mismatches trigger manual review.
- [ ] **ID image quality**: driving licence photo — all 4 corners visible,
      no glare, not expired.
- [ ] **Business description is concrete**: "SaaS — API subscriptions for
      web scraping / Markdown conversion" passes review faster than vague
      descriptions like "tech services".

## Step 1 — Escalation email (send after the 48h mark)

**To:** support@paystack.com
**Subject:** `Activation follow-up — [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] — submitted [DATE]`

> Hi Paystack team,
>
> I submitted my business activation on [DATE] (business name:
> [YOUR BUSINESS NAME], account email: [YOUR PAYSTACK EMAIL],
> business type: Starter Business, Kenya).
>
> I've verified that all Compliance sections show complete and I haven't
> received any follow-up questions. Could you confirm:
>
> 1. Current status of the activation review
> 2. Whether anything further is needed from my side
> 3. Expected remaining timeline
>
> My product is live and customers are ready to pay, so speed matters a
> great deal. Happy to provide any additional documents instantly.
>
> Thank you,
> [YOUR NAME]
> [PHONE NUMBER]

## Step 2 — Parallel nudges (same day)

- **X/Twitter:** post/DM **@PaystackHelp** or **@paystack**: "Submitted
  activation [DATE], all compliance sections complete, no feedback received
  yet — could someone take a look? Account email: [yours]" — their social
  team is responsive and this publicly pings the account.
- **Dashboard widget:** bottom-right chat bubble (if available on your plan)
  — same message.
